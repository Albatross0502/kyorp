The Manifesto

Key to Original Purity

KYORP India Private Limited was established on a radical standard: that holistic Indian healing principles must not merely coexist with modern bioscience, but cross-validate and elevate it.

01 / The Core Formula

Ayurveda × Modern Science × Clinical Validation × Original Purity

Too often, Ayurvedic products trade on romance without clinical rigour; conventional dermocosmetics prioritize synthetic quick-fixes while ignoring systemic equilibrium. KYORP resolves this tension by deploying standardized botanical extracts alongside verified biomimetic peptides, ceramides, and vitamins.

Ecosystem, Not a Single Brand

Rather than forcing baby care, clinical dermatology, oral hygiene, and sexual wellness into a confusing single catalog, KYORP is architected into 15 dedicated divisions. Each maintains its own clinical authority and product rigor while upholding master purity benchmarks.

Uncompromising Original Purity

Original Purity means no adulteration, zero heavy metals, single-source botanical harvesting from certified organic terroirs in Kashmir, Kerala, and Himachal Pradesh, verified through batch HPLC analysis.
